# FOCUS OS • OPERATIONAL PROTOCOL
> **Strategic Execution Terminal v2.0**

Welcome to Focus OS. This guide outlines the standard operating procedures for achieving maximum tactical efficiency using the Obsidian Aurora workstation.

---

## 01 • IDENTITY VERIFICATION
Access to the Command Center is restricted to authorized personnel.

1. **Uplink:** Navigate to the `/login` portal.
2. **Handshake:** Enter your operator email and access key (integrated from your Portfolio system).
3. **Establish Link:** The system will simulate a neural handshake. Once verified, you will be redirected to the Primary Dashboard.

---

## 02 • MISSION INITIALIZATION
Missions (Sprints) are the core of the Focus OS framework.

1. **Design Phase:** If no active mission is detected, you will be prompted to "Initialize Mission".
2. **Codename:** Assign a high-impact name to your mission (e.g., *PROJECT QUANTUM*).
3. **Target:** Define the single most important objective to achieve by the end of the duration.
4. **Neural Protocols:** Design your week by adding recurring daily tasks. These are the "rules" of your mission (e.g., *Deep Work*, *Neural Optimization*, *System Build*).
5. **Deployment:** Select your temporal duration (7D to 90D) and click **Establish Secure Uplink**.

---

## 03 • DAILY TACTICAL EXECUTION
The **Tactical List** is your daily battle plan.

1. **Active HUD:** View your primary mission parameters and metrics in the central cockpit.
2. **Objective Verification:** As you complete your designed protocols, check them off in the list.
3. **Efficiency Index:** Your "Output" score in the Metrics HUD will update in real-time. Aim for a >80% index to maintain streak integrity.
4. **Sticky Intel:** Keep an eye on the **Neural Feed** and **Integrity** sensors on the left as you execute.

---

## 04 • STRATEGIC RESERVE
The **Intel Node** (Parking Lot) is for non-mission distractions.

1. **Concept Capture:** When a new idea interrupts your focus, stashed it immediately in the **Strategic Reserve**.
2. **Categorization:** Tag entries as *Project*, *System*, or *Protocol*.
3. **Archive:** This keeps your current mission focused while ensuring no potential "Intel" is lost.

---

## 05 • WEEKLY REFLECTION
Every Sunday, the **Strategic Reflection** module unlocks.

1. **Initialize Debrief:** Click "Begin Protocol" in the Weekly Review module.
2. **Success Parameters:** Record what worked well within your current system.
3. **System Failures:** Honestly name the bottlenecks and protocol breaks.
4. **Primary Directive:** Define the "One Thing" to focus on for the upcoming week.
5. **Secure Log:** Committing the review archives the data and adjusts system parameters for next week.

---

## 06 • SYSTEM INTELLIGENCE
Analyze your long-term execution data.

1. **Intel Module:** Navigate to the **Data** icon in the Dock to view your **Neural Output Heatmap**.
2. **Peak Tactical:** Identify your high-performance days (pure teal/white) vs. low-signal days.
3. **Mission Archives:** Use the **Logs** icon to review past codenames, goals, and deployment windows.

---

## 07 • HUD CUSTOMIZATION
Optimize the workstation for your environment.

1. **Solar/Stealth Toggle:** Use the Sun/Moon icon in the Vertical Dock to switch between **Cloud Aurora** (Light) and **Obsidian Aurora** (Dark) modes.
2. **Personnel Node:** Navigate to the **User** icon to adjust **HUD Opacity** or toggle **Neural Sync Alerts**.
3. **Cache Protocol:** If data feels out of sync, use the "Purge Sync Cache" button in your profile.
